Privacy Policy

Lilla Företagshjälpen is designed to run locally on the user’s device. The application does not collect, track, or transmit personal data to the developer or to any third party.

Personal data

All information entered by the user (such as bookkeeping data, records, and notes) is stored locally on the user’s device. The developer has no access to this data.

Internet and cloud services

Lilla Företagshjälpen does not require an internet connection for its core functionality. If the user chooses to export or upload files to a cloud service or external storage, this is done entirely at the user’s own discretion and responsibility, in accordance with the selected service’s terms and privacy policy.

Third-party services

The application does not use third-party services for analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection.

Children

Lilla Företagshjälpen is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated if the application changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.
